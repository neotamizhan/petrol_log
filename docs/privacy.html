Privacy Policy

Vehicle Logbook is designed as an offline, local-first app. It does not create accounts, operate a backend, collect analytics, run ads, or sell personal information.

Effective: May 3, 2026 Version: 1.0.0+3

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Vehicle Logbook handles information when you use the app or contact support. The short version: your vehicle records stay on your device unless you choose to share them outside the app.

Vehicle Logbook is developed and maintained by the Vehicle Logbook developer. For privacy questions, contact neotamizhan@gmail.com.

Vehicle Logbook has no account system, no cloud sync service, no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK, and no crash reporting SDK.

Information You Enter

The app lets you enter and manage vehicle ownership records, including:

  • Vehicle details such as name, make, model, year, plate number, and odometer.
  • Fuel records such as date, odometer reading, fuel type, cost, and notes.
  • Maintenance records such as service type, category, date, odometer, cost, next due date, next due odometer, and notes.
  • Preferences such as currency, fuel types, fuel prices, selected vehicle, selected fuel type, and theme mode.

This information is stored locally by the app. The developer does not receive it through the app.

Local Storage

Vehicle Logbook stores app data on your device using platform-provided local storage. The app does not provide hosted backup, hosted sync, or a web account where your vehicle records are uploaded.

Your operating system or device backup settings may independently back up app data. Those backups are controlled by Apple, Google, your device manufacturer, or your own backup provider, not by Vehicle Logbook.

CSV Import

If you use CSV import, the app asks you to select a CSV file. The selected file is read and parsed locally to create fuel records. Vehicle Logbook does not upload the CSV file or its contents to the developer.

The app may request file or media-library access only when required by the operating system file picker. That access is used to let you choose an import file.

Data Collection and Sharing

Vehicle Logbook does not collect, sell, rent, or share your vehicle records, fuel history, maintenance history, or usage behavior with third parties.

The app does not use third-party analytics, third-party advertising, tracking pixels, or social login. It does not transmit app usage telemetry to the developer.

If you email support, your email address, message, attachments, and any details you choose to include will be used to respond to your request. Email processing is handled by the email providers involved in sending and receiving the message.

Permissions

Vehicle Logbook is intended to request only the access needed for its features. File access may be used for CSV import. The app does not require location, contacts, microphone, camera, advertising ID, or background tracking permissions for its core features.

Retention and Deletion

Because records are stored locally, they remain on your device until you edit or delete them, clear the app data, or uninstall the app. The developer cannot remotely view, recover, or delete records stored only on your device.

For practical deletion guidance, see the Data Control and Deletion page.

Children's Privacy

Vehicle Logbook is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the app changes or when legal, platform, or operational requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, support requests, or data-control questions, email neotamizhan@gmail.com.
