Data Control and Deletion

Vehicle Logbook does not create user accounts or store your vehicle records on developer-controlled servers. This page explains how to control local app data.

Last updated: May 3, 2026 No server account required

Summary

There is no Vehicle Logbook account to delete. The app stores records locally on your device. The developer cannot remotely access, export, recover, or delete records that are stored only on your device.

If an app store asks for an account deletion URL, this page documents that Vehicle Logbook does not offer account creation and therefore has no hosted account deletion flow.

Delete Records in the App

  1. Open the relevant list. Use the home timeline, maintenance screen, vehicles screen, or settings screen depending on the data type.
  2. Select the item you want to remove. Open a fuel record, maintenance entry, vehicle, or fuel type.
  3. Use the delete action. Confirm the deletion prompt. Some items with history may be archived or marked inactive to preserve record integrity.

Clear All Local App Data

To remove all local Vehicle Logbook data from a device, use your operating system's app data controls or uninstall the app. The exact labels vary by platform.

iPhone or iPad

Deleting the app typically removes local app data from the device. Device or iCloud backup behavior is controlled by your iOS settings.

Android

Use the system App Info screen to clear storage for Vehicle Logbook or uninstall the app. Device backup behavior is controlled by Android and your account settings.

macOS or web builds

Remove the app and clear associated local app storage through the platform controls available for that installation.

CSV Files

CSV import files remain wherever you stored them. Deleting app records does not delete the original CSV file from your device, cloud drive, email, or file manager. Delete those files separately if needed.
