Common help topics

Most issues are tied to selected vehicle context, CSV formatting, or local device storage.

Records appear under the wrong vehicle

Check the selected vehicle on the home screen before adding a record. Existing records stay associated with the vehicle selected when they were created.

CSV import fails

Use a header row and include at least Date, Odometer, and Cost columns. Supported date formats include yyyy-MM-dd, dd/MM/yyyy, MM/dd/yyyy, and dd-MM-yyyy.

Fuel volume looks unexpected

Vehicle Logbook estimates volume from cost and the selected fuel type's price per liter. Confirm the price and currency in Settings.

Maintenance status is overdue

A service can be overdue by date or odometer. Add a newer maintenance entry for the same vehicle and schedule after the work is completed.

Data backup

The app stores data locally and does not provide cloud sync. Before uninstalling, replacing a phone, or clearing app data, make sure your device backup plan covers app data.

Privacy questions

Read the Privacy Policy and Data Control pages for details on local storage, CSV import, deletion, and support email handling.

Before sending a CSV import issue

  1. Confirm the required columns. Date, Odometer, and Cost are required. Notes and Fuel Type are optional.
  2. Check date formatting. Try yyyy-MM-dd for the clearest import result.
  3. Remove currency symbols for testing. Cost values should parse as plain numbers. Thousands separators are accepted in many cases, but simple numbers are best.
  4. Send only a small sample if you need help. A few representative rows are usually enough. Remove plate numbers, notes, or other personal details before sharing.
