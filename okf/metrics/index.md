# Concepts

* [Maintenance Due Status](maintenance-due-status.md) - Per-schedule maintenance status (overdue, due soon, on track, completed) evaluated only for the newest record in each schedule.
* [Overall Stats](overall-stats.md) - Aggregate fuel and cost statistics computed over the filtered set of fuel fill records.
* [Refill Forecast](refill-forecast.md) - Weighted-average prediction of the next refill date with a confidence score derived from refill-interval consistency.
