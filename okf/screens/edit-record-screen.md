# Purpose

The [AddRecordScreen](/screens/add-record-screen.md) form pre-filled from an existing
[FillRecord](/models/fill-record.md), with a Delete action. Reached by tapping a fuel
entry on the [HomeScreen](/screens/home-screen.md) timeline. Save/Delete call
`updateRecord` / `deleteRecord` on the [RecordsProvider](/state/records-provider.md).
