# Purpose

Lists all [Vehicle](/models/vehicle.md) entries with status indicators. The FAB opens
[AddVehicleScreen](/screens/add-vehicle-screen.md); tapping a vehicle opens
[EditVehicleScreen](/screens/edit-vehicle-screen.md). Reached from the
[HomeScreen](/screens/home-screen.md) vehicle switcher.
